Funding & open interest —

loading…
TradFi perps settle funding every 8h, capped at ±1.00%, interest component 0%. A 1% funding print costs leverage × 1% of your margin — at 20x that is a fifth of it, so carry is not a rounding error.

Survivable sizing work backwards from the liquidation

Leverage sets liquidation distance; size does not. So the first number is the maximum leverage at which normal daily range doesn't reach your liquidation — that's the only lever that moves it. The second is position size from your stop and max loss, which is a separate constraint. Take the smaller of the two.
