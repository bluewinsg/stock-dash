Basis = (mark − index) ÷ index. Z-score is against the last ~500h of that symbol's own premium history, so it says "wide for this contract", not wide in the abstract. When the cash market is shut the index is frozen at the last print — basis then measures how far the perp has run ahead of a stale reference, which is what resolves into the 09:30 ET open.
